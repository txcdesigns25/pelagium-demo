Frequently asked questions

What is a fractional CFO?

A fractional CFO is a senior finance executive who works with your company part-time, typically a set number of days per month, providing CFO-level forecasting, reporting, cash management and strategy without the cost of a full-time hire.

Who does Pelagium Capital work with?

U.S. lower middle market companies, generally owner-led businesses with roughly $5M to $100M in revenue, that need stronger financial leadership, are preparing to raise growth capital, or are getting ready for a sale or recapitalization.

How do CFO advisory and capital raising work together?

Advisory gets you investor-ready: clean financials, a defensible forecast and a clear story. Capital raising then takes that package to the right lenders and investors. Many clients start with advisory and move into a raise.
